# Incident Report: INC-7001

### Severity
**Severity:** High  
**Confidence:** 0.85  
**Rationale:** The logs and metrics indicate significant error rates and degradation in service performance following a recent deployment, leading to major user-facing errors.

### Timeline  
- **14:02Z:** Spike in HTTP 502/503 responses begins, accounting for ~42% of requests.  
- **13:58Z:** Recent deployment of version 2.14.0 made, migrating the PaymentGateway.  
- **14:17Z:** Incident response initiated; service checks reveal high rates of HTTP 5xx errors (1,842 total).  
- **14:20Z:** Checkout service restarted to remediate issues caused by the deployment.  
- **14:25Z:** Service stabilizes, error rates decrease back to baseline levels.  

### Root-Cause Hypothesis  
The increase in HTTP 502/503 responses appears correlated with the deployment of version 2.14.0. The new PaymentGateway client likely introduced compatibility or configuration issues leading to increased errors and upstream timeouts.

### Remediation Performed  
The checkout service was restarted to clear any transient issues and restore normal operation.  

### Follow-Ups  
1. Review and assess the changes introduced in version 2.14.0 to identify possible improvements or mitigation strategies.  
2. Increase monitoring for payment processing related issues to ensure stability.  
3. Conduct a post-mortem with the development team to address deployment processes.
